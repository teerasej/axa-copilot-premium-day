# Practice files

All files contain synthetic information for Asteria Insurance, a fictional company. They do not contain AXA operational or participant data.

## Download all files

[Download the complete practice pack](/files/Asteria_Copilot_Premium_Practice_Files.zip)

After downloading, extract the ZIP and upload the eight files to your `Asteria Copilot Workshop` folder in OneDrive.

## Download individual files

| File | Use in the workshop |

| [Asteria_Operational_Context.docx](/files/Asteria_Operational_Context.docx) | Evidence, business context, and decision questions |

## File-handling rule

Keep the filenames unchanged. Work on your own copies and retain the originals so you can recover if an edit goes wrong.
